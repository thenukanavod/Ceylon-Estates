HISTORY

Ceylon Estates started with a simple idea: finding land, a house or an apartment in Sri Lanka should be clear, honest and easy. We began by listing a handful of properties and have grown step by step, adding local area information, a network of mortgage brokers and listings from more districts as buyers and sellers asked for them.

Journey

Ceylon Estates brings lands, houses and apartments together with the finance and local knowledge that go with them. Every step we take is guided by one goal: to help people find the right place and move in with confidence.

QUALITY POLICY

We aim to delight our customers with accurate listings, clear prices and fast, friendly support. Every listing is reviewed before it is published, and we keep improving the site based on what buyers and sellers tell us.

CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY

We believe a good property business should be fair to everyone. We are committed to honest advertising, to protecting the personal information of our users, and to supporting the communities and neighbourhoods we list in.
